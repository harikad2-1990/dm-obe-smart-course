01 / COURSE OVERVIEW

Learn the methods. Question the evidence.

Discover patterns

Explore data types, descriptive statistics, similarity, frequent itemsets, associations, and correlations.

Build predictions

Compare decision trees, Bayesian methods, Bayesian belief networks, backpropagation, and support vector machines.

Interpret responsibly

Compare clustering methods, evaluate cluster quality, and consider privacy and societal impact.

02 / COURSE OUTCOMES

Five outcomes, one connected workflow

CO1 · Understand

Understand basic data-mining concepts, data types, statistical measures, and data-similarity techniques.

CO2 · Apply

Apply methods to discover frequent patterns, associations, and correlations in data.

CO3 · Apply

Use classification techniques like decision trees, Bayesian methods, and SVM for prediction.

CO4 · Apply / Analyze

Perform clustering using different methods and evaluate cluster quality.

CO5 · Understand

Understand recent trends, applications, and societal impact of data mining.
